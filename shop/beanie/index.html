Why we recommend this piece

The Yupoong 1501KC is our default beanie because it works for people who don't usually wear beanies. Cuff stays folded neatly, knit is warm but not itchy, and the fit doesn't squeeze your head into an oval.

We stock these for Texas winter events, ski-trip giveaways, HVAC crews wintering trucks, church youth ski retreats, and anyone doing an outdoor market November through February.

How we decorate it

Embroidery on cuff — The default — centered logo on the folded cuff, ~2-3 inches wide. Reads clean because the cuff is a natural print zone. $10–16 depending on stitch count.

Embroidery on crown — Above the cuff, on the body of the beanie. Bigger logo real estate. $14–20.

Woven patch — Sewn-on patch for a premium retail feel. Works great for detailed multi-color designs. $12–18.

Frequently asked

Does it come in other colors?

Yes — Yupoong carries ~20 colors, including team colors like maroon, navy, forest green, and heathers. Kristen will confirm availability.

Can I have a leather patch instead of embroidery?

Yes — woven or leather patches are an option for a premium retail feel. Pricing similar to embroidery.

How long does a run take?

Embroidery: 5–10 business days. Patches: 7–12 days depending on patch vendor turnaround.

What about a lined winter version?

We can source fleece-lined beanies for outdoor crew work in colder climates. Ask in the quote if you need extra warmth.
