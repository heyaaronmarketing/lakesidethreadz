Why we recommend this piece

The Bella 3001 is our top-shelf blank when you want a shirt that fits like something you'd actually buy retail — soft, structured, honest. Airlume combed cotton means no pilling; ringspun jersey means it hangs cleanly instead of billowing.

We recommend it for anything customer-facing: church staff, event teams, chamber gifts, reunion runs, school spirit weeks. If the person wearing it will actually keep wearing it, this is the shirt.

How we decorate it

Screen print — Best for bold single-color or 2-3 color designs, especially at 25+ pieces. Sharp, durable, wash-tested. $8–14 per piece depending on colors and print size.

DTF transfer — Full-color photo-quality artwork the screen can't reproduce. Slight glossy hand, ideal for detailed logos, portraits, and gradient designs. $10–16 per piece.

Embroidery — Adds a premium retail feel — usually left-chest logos. $12–22 depending on stitch count.

Frequently asked

How is this different from a Gildan 5000?

The Bella 3001 has a softer hand and a more modern retail cut — think Target apparel section. The Gildan 5000 is heavier, boxier, workwear-oriented. Both are great; they just fit different scenarios.

Which colors do you stock?

Bella carries 100+ colors — solids, heathers, and tri-blends. Kristen will send options in her quote based on your artwork.

How long does a run take?

Screen print: 7–14 business days from proof approval. DTF: 5–10 days. Embroidery: 5–10 days. Rush turnaround is available for time-sensitive events — ask in the quote.

What about kids' sizes?

Yes — Bella has youth versions we can add to the same order so families match. Mention it in the quote.
