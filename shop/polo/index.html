Why we recommend this piece

The Core 365 88181 is the polo we recommend for teams that need to look professional in 95° heat and don't want to babysit their shirts. Textured interlock wicks sweat, resists wrinkles when it comes out of the dryer, and holds a logo like it was born for it.

Chamber staff wear these to member events. Real-estate teams wear them to open houses. Golf tournaments hand them out and people actually keep wearing them a year later. Not fancy; just correct.

How we decorate it

Embroidery — The default for polos — left-chest logo, 3–4 inches, stitched directly into the interlock. Looks tailored. $14–24 depending on stitch count.

DTF transfer — When your logo has gradients or fine detail embroidery can't hold, DTF gets the full-color version onto the polo cleanly. $12–18.

Sleeve or side embroidery — Small logo on the sleeve or hem for a subtler look. Popular for adding sponsor logos on team polos.

Frequently asked

Do you carry a women's cut?

Yes — Core 365 88181 is the unisex cut; 78181 is the women's fitted version. We can run both on the same order so the team matches.

Can this be embroidered with multiple logos?

Yes — left-chest is standard; we can add a sleeve logo or back-yoke text on the same shirt. Mention it in the quote.

How long does a run take?

Embroidery: 5–10 business days from proof approval. DTF: 5–10 days. Rush turnaround is available for time-sensitive events.

What colors?

Core 365 offers ~15 solid colors including safety yellow/orange for outdoor crews. Kristen will confirm current availability.
