How it works

1

Send us your artwork

Email your school logo (or wordmark, or spirit graphic) with a note on what you want it on. If you don't have art we can trace, we'll help.

2

Kristen writes a real quote

By hand, usually same day. Pricing tied to your quantity, decoration type, and any bulk-order breaks.

3

Approve a digital proof

We send a mockup of exactly what will be printed or stitched. Sign off before we make anything.

4

Pickup or ship

5–10 business day turnaround from proof approval. Pickup on Twin Creeks Dr, or we can ship to a booster/parent-group coordinator.

Every quote written by hand, by Kristen

No account. No calculator that spits out fake pricing. When you send us your Trinity Tigers artwork, Kristen (the owner, and the person actually stitching your gear) sits down and writes you a real quote — usually the same day, always within one business day.

Booster clubs, athletic directors, parent groups, PTA orders, senior-class shirts — we've done all of it. If it's a bulk run, ask about tier pricing at 24, 48, and 96+ pieces.
