Custom Embroidery in Texas

Hand-stitched by Kristen in Livingston. Shipping statewide, no minimums, real quotes written by hand.

Get Your Quote →

Why Texas businesses choose us for embroidery

We run a small embroidery shop on Lake Livingston. The whole operation is Kristen at the machine, writing every quote by hand, stitching every piece, and packing it herself. There's no account manager, no sales rep, no outsourcing to an overseas fulfillment center. For businesses in Houston, Austin, Dallas, San Antonio, and everywhere in between, that means the person stitching your logo is the person who answers the phone when you call to confirm thread colors.

What we embroider for Texas customers

Left-chest logos on polos and oxford shirts for office staff. Front-crown logos on Richardson 112 trucker hats for crews, teams, and boosters. Puff embroidery on structured caps for a bolder raised look. Full-color patches sewn onto work shirts for HVAC and contractor crews. 3D puff on front panels of dad hats for boutique brands. Small monograms on jackets, gift pieces, and wedding-party apparel. If it can hold a needle, we can stitch it.

Turnaround + shipping statewide

Standard turnaround is 5-10 business days from proof approval. We ship anywhere in Texas via USPS or UPS and include tracking. Rush orders (2-3 days) are available for time-sensitive events — mention your deadline in the quote and Kristen will confirm if it's workable. Pickup at our Twin Creeks Drive location in Livingston is always free.

No-minimum pricing that scales

We quote single pieces and we quote 500. There's no 12-piece minimum, no setup fee for digitizing a logo you already own, and tier pricing kicks in at 12, 24, 48, and 96 pieces. Expect roughly $8-15 per polo for a simple left-chest logo in Texas-reasonable quantities, $18-28 per embroidered hoodie depending on stitch count, $12-20 per trucker hat. Final numbers are always sent by hand after Kristen reviews your artwork.

Frequently asked

Do you serve all of Texas or only East Texas?

We ship statewide. Most of our business is within 50 miles of Lake Livingston (Polk, San Jacinto, Walker, Trinity counties) where we can offer same-week pickup, but we regularly ship embroidery to Houston, Austin, Dallas, San Antonio, Lubbock, El Paso, and everywhere in between.

What's the minimum order for custom embroidery?

None. We'll stitch one polo, one hat, or one hoodie if that's what you need. Tier pricing starts at 12 pieces and gets meaningfully better at 48 and 96.

Do you charge a digitizing fee for new logos?

Not if you already have embroidery-ready art (DST, EMB, or clean vector). If we need to digitize from a raster logo, it's a one-time $25-50 depending on complexity, and we keep the digitized file on hand for every future reorder.

How long does a Texas embroidery order take?

Standard is 5-10 business days from the time you approve the mockup. Rush orders can land in 2-3 days for an extra 25% — mention your deadline in the quote and Kristen will tell you what's realistic.

Can you match my corporate thread colors?

Yes. We stock Madeira Polyneon and Isacord thread lines (3,000+ colors between them), and we'll pull PMS matches from either line. If you have a brand guide, send it with your art.

Do you do puff embroidery / 3D embroidery?

Yes — we run foam-front puff embroidery on trucker hats and structured caps. Simple block letters and solid mascot shapes puff cleanly; thin lines and gradients don't translate well. Kristen will flag it if your logo isn't a puff candidate.

Ready to get a real quote?

Kristen writes every quote by hand — usually same day, always within one business day. No account required, no bots.
