Booster Club Apparel for Texas Schools

A booster club store without the headache. Low minimums, parent-group ordering forms, same-season turnaround.

Running a booster-club apparel run without losing a weekend

Booster-club apparel runs are supposed to raise money for the program. They're not supposed to eat 15 hours of a volunteer parent's weekend chasing sizes, collecting Venmo, and assembling an Excel sheet of who ordered what. We've been doing this for Lake Livingston-area schools for a few years and the process is as hands-off as we can make it for the booster president.

How a typical booster run works with us

Step one: you send us your school's licensed mark (or let us pull it from our file if we've already done a run for your school). Step two: we build you a simple ordering page (Google Form or a shared link) with the pieces you want to offer, sizes YXS through 4XL, colors in your school's palette, and the per-piece price you want to charge families. Step three: the ordering window stays open for 2-3 weeks. Step four: Kristen stitches everything and ships it to the booster president in one box, with a per-order packing slip so you can hand pieces out at a parent-pickup night. That's it. You never touch an Excel sheet.

Pieces that work well for booster runs

Trucker hats (always the first sellout), team hoodies with sleeve logos, long-sleeve dri-fit tees for game days, cuffed beanies for playoff football weather, mom + dad polo lines for the parent section, youth sizes for little siblings. We carry small-run blanks that make 12-piece orders cost-effective.

The margin for your booster club

We quote you wholesale (per-piece embroidered cost), and you set the retail price on the ordering page. Typical boosters mark up 50-100% depending on the piece. For a 100-piece fundraiser at $12 wholesale polos sold at $22, that's $1,000 clear for the program — not huge, but reliable, and the gear actually gets worn. We've seen booster clubs raise $4,000-8,000 on a single fall-sports open window.

Frequently asked

Do we need the school's permission to use the mark?

Yes — Texas districts require vendor approval for the licensed mark. Most of the time a simple email from the district's communications or athletics office is enough. We help booster presidents navigate this for first-time runs.

Can you set up an online ordering page for parents?

Yes. Simple form-based page with piece/size/color/qty, no app downloads, no accounts. Parents pay the booster club (Venmo, check, or PayPal — your call) and we collect the orders centrally when the window closes.

What's the minimum order for a booster fundraiser?

No minimum from us. Realistically, 25+ pieces across the run is where per-piece pricing becomes meaningful. We've run 12-piece varsity squad orders too.

How much can a booster run clear for our program?

Depends on your markup and volume. Rough example: a 100-piece run of embroidered polos at $12 wholesale sold at $22 retail = $1,000 profit. Hoodies clear more per piece. Hats clear the most as a % markup because wholesale is $8-10 and retail is $18-25.

How long does a booster run take start to finish?

Typical timeline: 1 week for setup + ordering page, 2-3 week ordering window, 10-14 days production after the window closes. Figure 5-6 weeks total from the day we start to the day the booster president has the boxes.

Do you do just athletic boosters, or band / FFA / Project Grad too?

All of it. Band booster t-shirts, FFA chapter polos, Project Grad senior class fundraiser shirts, drill team warmups, cheer camp apparel, choir tour pieces. The process is identical regardless of which program.

Ready to get a real quote?

Kristen writes every quote by hand — usually same day, always within one business day. No account required, no bots.
