Custom Fishing Shirts, Tournament & Hunting Apparel

Custom fishing shirts, tournament apparel, guide-service gear, and hunt-club shirts built for the water and the woods. Performance long-sleeves, embroidered hats, and everything in between — quoted same day.

Lake Livingston is fishing country, and East Texas is hunting country. At Lakeside Ink & Threadz, we create custom embroidered apparel for the outdoor community — fishing tournament teams, hunting clubs, guide services, and outdoor enthusiasts who want quality branded gear. We understand what works on the water and in the blind: moisture-wicking fabrics that dry fast, colors that look good in photos, and embroidery that holds up to sun, spray, and washing. Whether you need tournament shirts for your team or branded hats for your guide service, we're your local source in Onalaska.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you make tournament fishing shirts?

Absolutely — it's one of our specialties. We create custom tournament shirts with team logos, sponsor placements, and event branding for Lake Livingston and East Texas tournaments.

What fabrics work best for fishing shirts?

We recommend moisture-wicking performance fabrics that dry quickly and resist UV fading. They keep you comfortable on the water and hold up to repeated washing.

Can you add sponsor logos to tournament shirts?

Yes — we can place multiple sponsor logos on sleeves, chest, and back. Provide us the sponsor artwork and we'll handle the digitizing.

Do you embroider on camo?

Yes. We work with various camo patterns for hunting clubs and outdoor groups. Thread color selection is important to ensure visibility against the pattern.

Can you outfit a fishing guide service?

Yes — we create professional guide shirts, hats, and gear that look great in client photos and build your brand on the water.

How fast can you turn around tournament orders?

Standard turnaround is 7-10 business days. For tournaments with tight deadlines, contact us immediately — we can often accommodate rush orders.

Frequently asked

Can you embroider on Columbia PFG, Huk, and AFTCO fishing shirts?+

Yes — PFG (Performance Fishing Gear), Huk Performance, and AFTCO shirts embroider beautifully and are popular for guide services, marinas, and lake businesses around Lake Livingston. We use threads that hold color under sun and saltwater.

Can you do tournament team logos with sponsor patches?+

Yes — bass and crappie tournament teams routinely order matching shirts, hats, jackets, and bags from us. We can embroider your team logo and add sponsor patches in the standard tournament layout.
