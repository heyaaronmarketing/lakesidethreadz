How Much Does Custom Embroidery Cost? (2026 Prices)

Real 2026 pricing from a Texas embroidery shop — not 'contact us for a quote' theater. Per-piece ranges by garment, stitch count, and quantity.

Get Your Quote →

The quick ballpark

A left-chest embroidered logo on a mid-tier polo (Core 365, Port Authority) runs about $12-18 per piece in small quantities and drops to $8-12 per piece at 48+. A front-panel embroidered logo on a Richardson 112 trucker hat: $12-20 per piece small, $8-14 at volume. A standard left-chest logo on a quality hoodie (Bella+Canvas 3719): $22-32 per piece small, $16-24 at volume. These include the blank garment plus the embroidery.

What's actually in the per-piece price

Three things: the blank garment cost (usually 40-60% of the total), the embroidery labor (stitch time on the machine), and overhead (thread, bobbins, backing stabilizer, setup time per run). Good shops bake all three into one per-piece number so your quote is total cost, not a bait-and-switch.

One-time digitizing fee for new logos

If your logo has never been embroidered before, it needs to be converted from a raster or vector file into an embroidery-machine file (DST, EMB, etc.). That's a one-time $25-50 fee depending on logo complexity. Once digitized, the file is yours forever and every future reorder skips the fee. Shops that waive digitizing on first orders usually inflate per-piece pricing to cover it.

What makes price swing most

Stitch count is the biggest lever. A 3-inch left-chest logo at 7,000 stitches is one price; 15,000 stitches is 50-70% more. Thread colors used to matter (more colors = more thread changes = more labor); modern machines automate color changes so this is less of a factor now. Garment quality matters — a $4 blank Gildan polo embroiders for $5 (total: $9), a $11 blank Core 365 embroiders for $7 (total: $18). Quantity drops per-piece cost as setup time amortizes across more pieces.

Realistic price ranges by garment (2026)

Polo (left chest, 7-10k stitches): $12-18 single, $8-12 at 48+. T-shirt (left chest, ringspun cotton): $10-15 single, $7-11 at 48+. Trucker hat (foam front, 6-8k stitches): $12-20 single, $9-14 at 48+. Dad hat (3-inch front logo): $14-22 single, $11-17 at 48+. Hoodie (left chest): $22-32 single, $16-24 at 48+. Jacket (full-back embroidered name + logo): $45-75 single, $35-55 at 24+. Monogram on a towel or robe: $8-15 per piece, barely scales with quantity.

What inflates custom embroidery cost unfairly

Watch for these: hidden digitizing fees billed after the fact, 'setup fees' on top of per-piece pricing (should be one or the other, not both), rush fees over 50% of base pricing (25-35% is reasonable), per-color charges on modern machines (shouldn't happen), minimum order fees disguised as 'small order surcharges' (just raise the quote honestly). Any shop that can't give you a straight per-piece total in one number isn't being transparent.

Frequently asked

Is there a minimum order amount?

At Lakeside, no. We'll embroider a single polo. Tier pricing starts getting meaningfully better at 12 pieces. For most shops, minimums range from 1-24 pieces.

How is embroidery priced vs screen printing cost-wise?

At small quantities (1-24), embroidery and screen printing are close, with embroidery often cheaper because screen has per-color setup fees. At 50+ pieces of a simple 1-2 color design, screen printing is cheaper per piece. For logos on polos or hats, embroidery wins regardless of quantity.

Why does one shop quote me $15 and another $25 for the same shirt?

Three common reasons: different blank garments (same style, different brand or weight), different stitch counts (one shop's digitizer added more density), or different overhead structures (large shops have higher fixed costs). Ask each shop for a written quote that itemizes blank cost + embroidery cost + digitizing fee separately.

Does Lakeside charge a setup fee?

No setup fee per run. We charge a one-time digitizing fee ($25-50) if you're bringing a new logo. All future reorders of that logo have zero setup cost.

Can I get a real quote same day?

Yes. Text or email us a photo of your logo, garment type, quantity, and ideal delivery date. Kristen usually quotes same-day; always within one business day.
