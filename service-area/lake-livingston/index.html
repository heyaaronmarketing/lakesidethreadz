Serving Lake Livingston & East Texas

Custom embroidery, DTF printing, and team apparel — stitched right here on Lake Livingston and shipped or picked up across the region. One shop, real people, no middlemen.

Get a free quote Call (346) 988-5449

We’re a family-run embroidery and print shop based off Twin Creeks Dr in Livingston, TX. Most of our work is for local businesses, schools, teams, churches, marinas, and lake families across the greater Lake Livingston area and the surrounding East Texas counties. If you’re within about an hour of the lake, you’re in our normal delivery loop — otherwise we ship.

Here’s what’s near each town we serve and the kind of work that usually comes out of there. If your community isn’t listed, call anyway — we cover most of the piney-woods region between Houston and Nacogdoches.

Livingston

Our home base. Polk County seat, Livingston Chamber of Commerce, Livingston ISD Lions, and the community around US-59 and FM-1988. Lots of contractor polos, real-estate office branding, church shirts, and youth-sports uniforms come out of here.

Onalaska

On the west shore of Lake Livingston along US-190. Home to Onalaska ISD Wildcats, Yaupon Cove, and a bunch of lakefront businesses. Marina staff shirts, tournament gear, guide-service apparel, and lake-house family reunion tees are the usual asks.

Coldspring

San Jacinto County seat, gateway to the Sam Houston National Forest and the west side of the lake. Coldspring-Oakhurst Trojans, county-office polos, and volunteer fire department shirts are frequent orders from this side.

Point Blank

Small community on SH-190 right at the Trinity River bridge on the south end of the lake. We regularly do apparel for lake-adjacent short-term rentals, small marinas, and family gatherings here.

Trinity

West of the lake in Trinity County — home of Trinity ISD Tigers and the Westwood Shores community. Booster-club spirit wear and small-business embroidery are common from Trinity.

Huntsville

Walker County seat on I-45, home of Sam Houston State University and Huntsville ISD Hornets. We do a lot of team, booster, and small-business embroidery from Huntsville and Riverside — polos, hats, and crew apparel for local contractors.

Lufkin

Angelina County seat, the biggest city in deep East Texas. Lufkin ISD Panthers, Angelina College, and a lot of light-industrial and healthcare businesses. We handle employee-uniform programs and corporate embroidery orders that ship up to Lufkin regularly.

Cleveland

Liberty County, along US-59 on the way to Houston. Cleveland ISD Indians, plus a growing base of construction crews and service businesses. Work-shirt runs and crew polos are the usual orders from Cleveland.

Splendora

Montgomery County, right off US-59 north of Houston. Splendora ISD Wildcats and a bunch of contractor and trades businesses. We handle spirit wear, coach polos, and crew uniforms for the Splendora area.

Not sure if we cover your town?

Call or text Kristen. If we can’t reach you locally, we ship. Every quote and every proof comes from a real person — not a call center.
